Design the door

Ten doors at Palmetto Dental Group. Pick the lock, the reader, and the layout that keep people safe and keep the wrong people out.

How this challenge works
  1. Read the situation. The board shows the door, and the part marked ? is the one you choose.
  2. Pick an answer and press Check to see your points and why.
  3. Press Play (or Step and Back) to watch the best design work on the board: green when your answer was the best, coral when it needs review.
  4. Finish all ten rounds, review the results table, and try again.

Words to know: fail safe means the lock opens when power is lost; fail secure means it stays locked. REX means request to exit. The AHJ (authority having jurisdiction) is the official, often the fire marshal, who approves the design under the local building and fire code.
