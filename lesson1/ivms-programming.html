How to use this lab

  1. In the iVMS-4200 window below, open tab 1, Controller. Press + Add Device, type the values from the yellow work order note, and press Add. Success: the status says Online.
  2. Open tab 2, Door. Check the five values against the note and press Edit to save them. Then press Hardware Parameters Downloading to send them to the controller.
  3. Open tab 3, Person, and add the departments Clinical and Front desk, then the three employees. Open tab 4, Card, add cards 10045 to 10047, and press Issue Card for each one.
  4. Open tab 5, Schedule. Build the week plan Office hours by clicking hour blocks or typing From and To times. Then make the template Office hours with it.
  5. Open tab 6, Permission. Press + Add Permission and give both departments the staff entrance door with Office hours. Test one card on the test bench, then press Start Downloading.
  6. On the Test bench below, pick a card and a time and press Present card. Watch the board above. Then press Open door in tab 7, Status, read tab 8, Events, and do the fault drill.

      Things to notice

      • iVMS-4200 and the controller each keep their own copy of the settings. The controller decides with its copy, so a change counts only after Download Permission (or Hardware Parameters Downloading for the door settings).
      • A department permission covers everyone in that department, including people you add later, after the next download.
      • Door Magnetic must match the door contact. A normally closed contact goes with Remain Closed; a mismatch shows a closed door as open and raises door open timeout alarms.
      • A schedule's end time is not included: at 19:00 sharp, Office hours has already ended.
      • Remain open and Remain closed stay in effect until you send another anti-control command.
