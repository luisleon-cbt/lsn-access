How to use this lab

  1. Choose the lock under Panel setup below. Start with Electric strike, as on your lab sheet. After its test, come back and choose Magnetic lock.
  2. Connect the wires one at a time. On the board above, click a wire end, then click the terminal where it lands. Or choose a Wire end and a Terminal below and press Connect. The panel needs 18 wires.
  3. Press Check wiring. It names the first problem. Fix it, then check again until it says all 18 connections are right. Show hint tells you the fix, but each hint costs 2 points.
  4. Press Power on and test. Watch the board above and read each step under Test the door below. Your score out of 100 appears when the test ends.
  5. Press Fault hunt under Panel setup. A new technician wired that panel with one or two faults. Run the test, find the faults in the wire list, fix them, and test again.

  • Wire with the power off. The test turns the power on for you.
  • Drag on the board to turn it. Use +, −, the arrows, and FIT to zoom and move. Terminals and Door jump to those parts.
  • Keyboard: Space steps the test, B steps back, R runs or pauses, Esc cancels a pick.
  • This page does not save your wires. Finish a panel in one sitting.

      Things to notice

      • The relay is a dry contact: it only opens and closes. The 12 V for the lock comes from the supply into COM1.
      • The fail safe mag lock goes on NC1, so it has power at rest. The fail secure strike goes on NO1, so it gets power only when access is granted.
      • The band on the diode is the cathode. It goes to lock +. Put it in backward and it shorts the supply the moment the lock gets power.
      • Swap green and white and every 0 arrives as a 1. The controller reads a different number and the parity check fails.
      • On some DS-K2600 boards NO and NC share one terminal set by a jumper. The logic is the same.
